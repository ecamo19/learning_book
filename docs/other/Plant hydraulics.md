
Xylem become more specialized than tracheids by separating the water conducting tissue from the the mechanical support tissue. 


Water moves from one vessel to another laterally through bordered pits.

![Datacube concept](media/water_movement.png)


Vessel length is positively correlated with vessel diameter. In other words, the larger the vessel the wider the diameter. The functional significance of the greater number of short vessels is one of safety 

Conductivity = Flow rate per unit of area 
1/Conductivity =  Resistivity


Conductance = Flow rate
1/Conductance = Resistance


+ The Hagen-Poiseuille equation compute the relative conductivity of xylem vessels as: 

$$K_{capillary}\ =\ \frac{radius^4*\pi}{8n}$$
     where n is the dynamic viscosity of the liquid 


+ The conductance of all pipes in parallel is the sum of their individual conductances  

+ The total resistance of several pipes in series equals the sum of their individual resistances

Heat could mean a faster desiccation due to changes in water viscosity.
